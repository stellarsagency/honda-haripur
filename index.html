What is e:HEV?

Honda's innovative hybrid electric technology for a cleaner, more efficient drive

EV Drive Mode

During startup and city driving, the vehicle runs on the motor alone for zero-emission efficiency.

Hybrid Drive Mode

The motor runs using electricity generated by the engine for powerful acceleration.

Engine Drive Mode

During high-speed cruising, the engine directly drives the wheels for maximum efficiency.

Regenerative Braking

When decelerating, kinetic energy is converted to electricity and stored in the battery.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Honda vehicles

What is a Hybrid Electric Vehicle (HEV)?
A hybrid-electric vehicle (HEV) is equipped with electric motors in addition to an engine and achieves excellent fuel economy by efficiently using those two power sources.
How does an e:HEV work?
The e:HEV hybrid-electric system was created by combining all of the engine and electrification technologies Honda has amassed. It intelligently switches between three modes: EV drive, Hybrid drive, and Engine drive for optimal efficiency.
